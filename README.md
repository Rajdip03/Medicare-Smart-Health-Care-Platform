# 🏥 Medicare Smart Health Care Platform

A full-stack healthcare management platform designed to streamline interactions between **Patients**, **Doctors**, and **Hospital Administrators**. The system provides appointment booking, lab test management, prescription handling, AI-powered assistance, video consultations, and secure online payments in a centralized healthcare ecosystem.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Medicare Smart Health Care Platform digitizes hospital operations and improves patient care by providing:

* Online doctor appointment booking
* Video consultations
* Digital prescription management
* Lab test booking and tracking
* AI-powered healthcare assistance
* Hospital ward and bed management
* Secure authentication and authorization

The platform is divided into three major user roles:

* 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Portal
* 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Patient Portal
* 🛠️ Admin Portal

---
